VO LAM IDLE - TU DONG THU THAP TAI NGUYEN - PHIEN BAN KHONG CAN CHON TUNG TEP

1. Giai nen TOAN BO tap tin ZIP ra mot thu muc tren may co Internet.
2. Nhap dup TU_DONG_THU_THAP_TAI_NGUYEN.cmd.
3. De cua so hoat dong. Chuong trinh tu:
   - Kiem tra website.
   - Lay ca danh sach core va atlas/cong cu choi tu file manifest co san.
   - Bo qua tep da co va nhung tep xac nhan 404 o lan chay truoc.
   - Tai 4 luong, gioi han 4 yeu cau/giay, tu thu lai khi gap loi mang.
   - Dong goi file anh/am thanh moi thanh VoLamIdle_TAI_BO_SUNG_PHAN_01.zip ...
   - Viet BAO_CAO_TU_DONG.txt va DANH_SACH_CHUA_LAY_DUOC.txt.
4. Neu dung giua chung: chay lai cung tep CMD. Phan da tai duoc duoc giu nguyen.
5. Choi game offline bang CHAY_GAME_OFFLINE.cmd ngay trong thu muc.
6. Neu muon ChatGPT kiem tra va xuat lai ban offline da gom anh, gui vao chat:
   - BAO_CAO_TU_DONG.txt
   - Cac tep VoLamIdle_TAI_BO_SUNG_PHAN_XX.zip.
